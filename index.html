Why is all this stuff still up there?

Nobody forgot these things by accident. There are good reasons why they are still there.

Every kilogram counts

A rocket needs fuel to fly home. The heavier it is, the more fuel it needs. So astronauts took Moon rocks home instead of old equipment.

No pick-up service

Flying to Mars takes about seven months. Going back to collect an old robot would be far too hard and far too expensive.

Some of it still helps

A laser mirror on the Moon is still used today. And old equipment shows us what 50 years in space do to metal and plastic.

About this page: It was made for a challenge in the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026: “Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA’s Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars”. The idea is to tell stories about the equipment NASA has left on the Moon and Mars since the 1960s, so that it is not forgotten. The maps of the Moon and Mars and the pictures of the lost items are simplified drawings made for this page, but every landing site sits at its real coordinates.
